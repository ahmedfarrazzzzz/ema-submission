End-to-end user journey

One exit, from resignation to Form 16

Kavya Nair leads HR Operations at Northwind Labs, a fictional Bengaluru software company with about 2,100 people and 38 exits a month. The persona works in her Teams channel and talks to the person leaving by email.

Day −60

The HRIS approval triggers the persona. It maps every account and drafts the handover plan with the manager.
