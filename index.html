Offboarding Analyst: an AI employee that closes every exit in two days, not two months

When someone leaves, HR, IT, Payroll and their manager each own a piece of the exit, and nobody owns the whole. Access stays open, the final settlement takes weeks, and the employee chases their documents for months.

This persona doesn't hand anyone a checklist. Give it an employee ID and it does the work, and a person approves every step that touches money or access.

Built for India first (final settlement, gratuity, Form 16). The policy library makes it portable to other countries.

It works where each team already works

HR Ops

Gets a morning brief in Teams, approves plans in one place, and stops chasing five teams for inputs.

IT & Security

Sees every account, including ones outside SSO, and gets a timestamped evidence pack for auditors.

Payroll

Receives an audited settlement where every line cites a policy clause, ready to approve and pay.

The manager

Confirms named successors for repos, customers and approvals before anything is switched off.

The person leaving

Gets one clear email with their money, dates and documents, and can ask questions that only see their own record.
